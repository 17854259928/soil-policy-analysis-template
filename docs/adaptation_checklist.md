# Adaptation checklist

## Research scope

- Jurisdictions and policy systems:
- Time period:
- Languages:
- Soil-specific policies only, or policies with explicit soil mechanisms:
- Policy-design, implementation and evaluation documents included:
- Unit of analysis: document, policy, programme, intervention or provision:

## Corpus inclusion

- Define positive, negative and ambiguous examples.
- Define treatment of scientific studies, guidance and non-binding strategies.
- Define whether subnational policies are included.
- Define version control for amended or superseded legal acts.

## Ontology

- Review every objective and instrument with soil-policy experts.
- Add local policy instruments and terminology.
- Add inclusion, exclusion and borderline examples.
- Decide how multiple objectives and instruments are coded.
- Freeze and version the ontology before gold-standard annotation.

## Data structure

- Identify the minimum fields required by the research questions.
- Separate design, target, implementation, output and outcome variables.
- Specify units and metadata required for each soil indicator.
- Define unique identifiers and cross-document policy linkage rules.
- Define how conflicting values are retained.

## Validation

- Prepare representative multilingual and layout-diverse documents.
- Include documents with no soil-policy content.
- Double-code a gold-standard sample.
- Record inter-coder disagreement and adjudication decisions.
- Evaluate classification, field extraction, evidence attribution and numeric
  accuracy separately.

## Governance

- Confirm document licences and redistribution conditions.
- Remove credentials, personal information and restricted source documents.
- Record model, prompt, ontology and schema versions with every extraction run.
- Preserve original evidence and reviewer decisions.
