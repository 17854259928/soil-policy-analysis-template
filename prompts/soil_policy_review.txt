Review one proposed soil-policy record against its source evidence.

Check:

1. Scope: Is soil relevance explicit, secondary, or not demonstrated?
2. Identity: Are policy name, legal identifier and jurisdiction supported?
3. Temporality: Are adoption, implementation, target and reporting dates kept
   separate?
4. Instrument: Is the controlled code consistent with bindingness, payment
   design and implementation mechanism?
5. Results chain: Are planned targets, adoption outputs, measured outcomes and
   modelled effects separated?
6. Indicators: Are units, depth, baseline, spatial scale and method preserved?
7. Evidence: Does every positive or uncertain claim have a local quotation and
   locator?
8. Missingness: Has the record used null/empty values rather than speculation?
9. Conflicts: Are contradictory values or classifications retained for review?
10. Comparability: Does any harmonisation exceed what the source supports?

Return a list of confirmed fields, correction-required fields, missing evidence,
ontology ambiguities and recommended reviewer actions. Do not silently rewrite
the source record.
